Electrode Placement Guide

How to position the sensors ＋
Each EMG channel uses 2 sensors placed close together, plus a shared ground sensor near the ankle (numbers above match the channel, not the sensor color — see note below). In order:
1. Clean the skin at every electrode site before applying.
2. EMG 1 — Tibialis Anterior: front of the shin, upper third between the knee and ankle. Place both sensors close together, vertically, along the direction of the muscle fibers.
3. EMG 2 — Fibularis Longus: outer side of the calf, on the line from the fibular head (just below the outer knee) down toward the outer ankle bone. Place both sensors close together, vertically.
4. EMG 3 — Lateral Gastrocnemius: outer/back of the calf, upper third just below the knee. Place both sensors close together, vertically.
5. Ground — on the ankle bone (malleolus), inner and outer side. Bony, electrically quiet tissue away from the active muscles.
6. Secure the brace and confirm all channels show good signal quality.
Note: sensor cable/dot colors can vary between product batches. Always identify placement by the channel number (EMG 1/2/3) and muscle name above — not by color.
